## Interface conclusions

1. Ordinary/nonisolated: G01 and G06 demonstrably use distance to sets without isolation. G09 explicitly separates relative strong behavior; this is not license to substitute its strong branch. G02–G04 application details remain unverified.
2. q>1: G01 uses an inverse exponent convention. G05's geometric modulus and G08/G09's relative coderivatives cannot be read as superlinear residual gauges. No source in this lane has been verified to supply the full requested general combination; this is an evidence limitation, not an open-problem proof.
3. Exponent + modulus + radius: G01 Example 3.4 and G06 Theorem 3.3 provide existing explicit conditional certificates, at q<1 and q=1 respectively. They must be retained as controls against an overbroad “no quantitative ordinary certificates” claim. No inspected theorem extracts all three solely from unspecified finite partial operator data.
4. Target consistency: the symbols Γ, Θ, Ξ in G01 represent stationary sets; Ω,Θ in G08 represent restrictions/truncation of a multifunction. Equality of names does not imply equality of targets or error bounds. Any conversion to the actual T5 residual still requires a quantitative inequality in the correct direction.
5. Open status: R11 must remain O3/PENDING rather than author-certified open. G02 and G03 are not yet screened out. The broad R01 story should acknowledge recent generalized-subsmooth and relative-calculus advances while separating their actual scopes.
