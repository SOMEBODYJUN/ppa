\begin{theorem}
Let $F:\mathbb R\rightrightarrows\mathbb R$ have a nonempty closed graph and $F^{-1}(0)=\{0\}$.
Suppose that $\lambda,L,c,q>0$, $0<\gamma<1$, and $q\gamma>1$; that
$|u-v-\lambda(f-g)|\le L|u-v+\lambda(f-g)|^\gamma$
for all $(u,f),(v,g)\in\operatorname{gph}F$; and that
$|u|\le c\,r_F(u)^q$ for every $u\in\operatorname{dom}F$, where $r_F(u)=\inf_{f\in F(u)}|f|$.
Then there exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\subset\operatorname{ran}(I+\lambda F)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The all-pair estimate defines a continuous Cayley map on its natural input domain. Since $0$ is in that domain, continuity gives an open neighborhood of $0$ on which the map is defined. This proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
