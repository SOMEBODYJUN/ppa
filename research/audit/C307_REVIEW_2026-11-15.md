# C307 independent reception · 2026-11-15

## Verdict

**Major-potential conditional; not yet a theorem and not yet a four-journal result.**

Three independent passes converged on the same scope:

1. The bounded-step positive direction is a direct but valid extension of C302. For \(\lambda_k\in[\lambda_-,\lambda_+]\), the constants are \(1/(2\lambda_+)\) for sufficient decrease and \(1/\lambda_-\) for the relative-error estimate.
2. The real new contribution would be the non-quasianalytic converse for a schedule-specific function \(f_{(\lambda_k)}\), not the standard variable-step KL argument.
3. No simple algebraic counterexample was found. The decisive obligation is an explicit two-step-period construction for every compact connected \(K\), with a common Hessian margin \(\mu<1/\lambda_+\) and complete global-min resolvent coverage for both step sizes.

## Fatal wording restrictions

- Do not claim one function works for every schedule. A fixed input/output pair fixes the gradient and therefore fixes the step size in the resolvent equation.
- Do not allow \(\lambda_-=0\) in the theorem. The relative-error constant then disappears.
- Do not infer global coverage from a formal interpolated orbit. The lower Hessian margin must be established before calling the steps global minimizers.
- Do not treat the positive variable-step direction as the novelty center; bounded-step KL/ABS convergence is already standard.

## Mathematical handoff

For a proposed reverse chord \(z(t_k)\), use
\[
g_k=\frac{z(t_k)-z(t_{k+1})}{\lambda_k}.
\]
The two-step test must give a schedule-specific \(E^{(M)}\) jet interpolation, preserve the exact proximal equations at all sample points, and keep
\[
\nabla^2 f\succeq-\mu I,\qquad \mu<1/\lambda_+.
\]
If this test closes, the C302 fixed-step dichotomy becomes a schedule-stable regularity phase transition. If it fails, C307 must be reduced to the positive direction plus a scope-limited periodic construction.
