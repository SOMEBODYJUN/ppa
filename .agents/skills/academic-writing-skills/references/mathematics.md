# Mathematical writing and proof review

## Language and local scope

Use the requested output language. Default Chinese discussion to simplified Chinese; preserve established English terms where a translation would obscure meaning. In a surface edit, preserve mathematical spans, commands, labels, references, citations and quantifiers. State any semantic correction separately. Never translate identifiers or change an exponent just to improve wording.

For a section translation, preserve its mathematical content while repairing unclear sentence order. Before a material mathematical change, reconstruct and check the new statement. Keep process notes, reviewer replies, confidence scores and internal audit history outside the paper.

## Freeze the mathematical contract

Record the space, objects, domains, parameter ranges, quantifier order, target set, local neighborhood and scale. Distinguish complete operator graphs, localized graph blocks and selected algorithmic outputs. An existence hypothesis is not a conclusion of continuity or uniqueness.

For F set-valued, use the true residual r_F(y)=inf{||v||:v in F(y)}. A selected graph value gives only r_F(y)<=||v||. Do not reverse this inequality, identify the residual with a step, or use an error-bound gauge outside its domain. Check all actual outputs required by the claimed conclusion.

Distinguish all-pair graph conditions from solution-anchored conditions. All-pair reflected-resolvent estimates may imply uniqueness on the represented block; they do not supply input coverage or identify it with the complete resolvent. An allowed-subrelation theorem concerns every allowed selection, not arbitrary complete-fiber selections. Prove a full-fiber bridge if transferring the result.

## Verify a proof

1. State the exact version under review and each dependency.
2. Check object existence, nearest-point attainment, zero-distance cases, domain boundaries, multivalued fibers and every use of a local estimate.
3. Reconstruct the argument without importing missing assumptions. Check finite-prefix localization before using infinite summation or assuming an orbit remains in a certified neighborhood.
4. Attack the strongest assertion with simple degenerate or boundary examples; verify a proposed counterexample against every premise analytically.
5. Distinguish a complete proof, a conditional proof, a gap and a false claim. Finite simulations and symbolic/numerical checks support specific instances; they do not prove universal claims.

State counterexamples and fixes explicitly. Do not replace a false theorem by polished prose or silently add an assumption. Proof review may discover a valid proof; never invent evidence or present an unfinished scaffold as established mathematics.

## References and novelty

Use primary papers and exact versions for technical comparisons. Record the theorem number or page, source assumptions, and their mapping to the current objects. Separate input neighborhoods, output neighborhoods and graph-value truncation. Verify the whole conjunction needed by an imported theorem.

A failure of a displayed certificate does not exclude every theorem, reparameterization or alternative algorithm in that field. A stronger sufficient hypothesis set, one strict example, a known mechanism and a new theorem are different claims. Do not infer worldwide novelty from bounded search. Distinguish rate upper bounds from exact or sharp rates, and a usable constant from an optimal one.

## LaTeX and delivery

Preserve an existing functioning preamble. Use the manuscript's engine; use XeLaTeX or LuaLaTeX for Chinese when appropriate. Check environment balance, labels, references, citations, author identity and mathematical symbols. Compile the exact candidate and inspect rendered pages if a PDF is requested. Report unavailable compilation separately from content checks.

A review report should give concrete locators, severity, reasons and actionable repairs. A paper should contain the mathematics and its reader-facing explanation, not the review checklist. Keep one active manuscript and synchronize its title, abstract, introduction, conclusions and affected references after a material change.
