# Mathematical exposition and example selection

Start with the established object, algorithm and mathematical question. Introduce a new name only when it supports repeated use. For convergence papers, explain the ordinary interface first: a Cauchy orbit whose distance to a closed solution set tends to zero converges to a solution. Then show which estimates supply distance decay, summable displacement and local existence.

Put a focused literature account in the introduction. Organize sources by the questions they resolve and their actual assumptions; do not parade papers or claim an exhaustive survey after a bounded search. Keep the abstract for the object, principal result and its scope.

Begin proofs with the decisive identity, reduction or construction. Combine routine algebra when the inference remains visible. Preserve the existence, quantifier, true-residual and finite-prefix localization steps that carry the claim.

Give each example a specific job. Prefer familiar functions, geometric objects or algorithmic models when they retain the required properties. For a mechanism example, say what it isolates without presenting it as an established application. Present the object, the short calculation and the resulting insight; move repeated technical bounds to supplementary material.

Before replacing an example, freeze the properties it must preserve. Check non-Lipschitz behavior, sharp exponents, complete input coverage, true residuals, actual trajectories and the comparison it supports separately. A more pleasant monotone example need not establish the same theorem's reach. Update the abstract, comparisons and conclusion when the contribution changes.

Let the author's choices of objects, proof routes and material determine the voice. Use stable terminology and direct mathematical verbs. Remove repeated previews, repeated conclusions, generic significance claims and internal audit language. Keep necessary qualifications and failed converses once, beside the relevant statement.

Do not move a technical discussion into an unproved universal claim. A kernel change in a generalized proximal method changes the resolvent and the relevant residual. Establish the coordinate or lifting bridge before transferring convergence, finite length, topology or rates.

Read the exact revised passage as an ordinary mathematical explanation. Each paragraph must add identifiable information, and each statement must have the prerequisites the reader has already seen. Compile and inspect the rendered artifact when changing a formatted manuscript. Keep review records outside the paper.
