# Repository research and writing

The active DD1–DD2 task is to finish every item in the [acceptance checklist](research/audit/DD2_ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md) under the author's [original protocol](research/topics/dd2/DD2_FINAL_ACCEPTANCE_PROTOCOL.md). At the start of a new review session, read the [DD2 explanation and self-check](research/audit/DD2_PRO_START_HERE.md). Then work on the first unchecked item. Record its answer, evidence and independent verification in the checklist, then check, commit and push that item before taking the next one. Open and partial work remains unchecked. Use the [audit entry](research/audit/DD2_FINAL_ACCEPTANCE.md) for prior evidence. Deliver the completed checklist from origin/main only after the final checker passes.

Read RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md and relevant canonical definitions before mathematical changes. Keep exact claim versions, true residuals, graph-block versus complete-fiber scope, coverage and localization explicit. History is provenance, not a proof. Update canonical records when a substantive new checked claim is registered.

For author-directed mathematical paper outlines, use `$paper-skeleton-and-logical-architecture` at `.agents/skills/paper-skeleton-and-logical-architecture`, downloaded from VeryMath/AI4Math-Writing. Present the actual definitions, lemmas, assumptions, theorems and corollaries in the author's order. Apply new instructions cumulatively: preserve previously requested definitions and results unless the author explicitly removes them. Validate the final outline against each author instruction, not only against correct formulas. Maintain one current outline and one runnable verifier with its current output; do not create duplicate chapter drafts, review ledgers or revision reports for routine outline edits. Delete superseded process files rather than archiving additional copies.

Use `$academic-writing-skills` at `.agents/skills/academic-writing-skills` for Chinese/English mathematical exposition, LaTeX, proofs or referee review when its corresponding mode is needed. No mandatory multi-skill pipeline. Keep editorial/process reports under `发表成果整理` and reader-facing exposition in the manuscript. The active source for the author-directed DD paper is `发表成果整理/DD`, following `research/manuscripts/dd2/DD2_outline_and_pro_handoff.md`. The earlier `发表成果整理/RLEB` paper remains independent; `原始投稿包` is immutable input.

For prose editing, use `$humanizer` at `.agents/skills/humanizer` (MIT, https://github.com/blader/humanizer) alongside mathematical writing when requested. Apply its style checks only to prose; preserve scientific scope, notation and necessary qualifications. Do not promise an AI-detector score. For full-manuscript work requested with subagents, require independent mathematical and language reading of the exact final source before committing. Reuse current records instead of adding review-report copies.
